My trip of learning

Hello everybody, and welcome to my space! I am Jeffer Romero, and this blog is a bitacora of my emotional trip to dominate English as a second language. Along this road, I have discovered that learning a language goes so far beyond the grammatical rules; it is an immersion in new cultures, ideas, and forms of seeing the world.

Here I will share how I have taken advantage of videos, text, audio, and many other tools to improve my skills in English. From everyday conversations to analysis of complex topics, each experience has been a forward step. Be ready to explore together! My Life in English: Explore themes day in and day out.
